## SOFTWARE REVIEW
**Google Sites: A Simple Website Builder**

Google Sites is a website builder that works in a web browser. It helps people create websites without writing code. Users can add pages, text, and photos, then publish their sites.

I like the simple page editor because it lets beginners focus on their content. A student can organize an introduction, add pictures, and check the layout before publishing. However, Google Sites has fewer options for custom designs and complex animations than a coded website.

I would recommend Google Sites to students who need a clear website for a class project. I will also compare it with other tools when I build another website.

## BLOG
**Drama Observation: A Midsummer Night's Dream**

Our drama was based on *A Midsummer Night's Dream* by William Shakespeare. It tells the story of four young lovers in Athens. Hermia loves Lysander, but her father wants her to marry Demetrius. Meanwhile, Helena loves Demetrius.

In the forest, Oberon asks Puck to use a magical love potion. Puck accidentally puts it on Lysander instead of Demetrius. Lysander wakes up and falls in love with Helena. Demetrius is enchanted later, and both men begin following Helena. Puck eventually corrects the mistake, and the couples can marry.

The wedding celebration also includes a short play called *Pyramus and Thisbe*. Its tragic love story is very different from the happy ending of the main play.

**My favorite characters** are Puck and Oberon. I enjoyed Puck's playful behavior and the funny misunderstandings he caused. Oberon also made the story interesting because he was the King of the Fairies.

**My favorite scene** was the confusion after the love potion changed Lysander's feelings. It became even funnier when both Lysander and Demetrius pursued Helena while Hermia was upset and confused.

I enjoyed seeing the costumes, expressions, and teamwork during the performance. Green leaves and light fabric decorated the stage like a forest. The drama helped me understand the story better. I also took some photos with friends and performers. It was a memorable experience for me.
